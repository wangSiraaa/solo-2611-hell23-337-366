# SDMX 2.1 subset
This fixture uses the standard message/structure/generic namespaces; this task does not claim full SDMX XSD conformance. Header/agency/concept metadata not needed by the declared contract are outside scope.
DataStructureComponents: three fixed dimensions and one PrimaryMeasure. LocalRepresentation/Enumeration/Ref selects a local Codelist. AttributeRelationship supports None, full non-time Dimension refs, Group ref, or PrimaryMeasure. assignmentStatus Mandatory means required.
GenericData supports one DataSet, Attributes/Value, SeriesKey, GroupKey, ObsDimension, ObsValue, and per-scope Attributes. Group @type identifies the DSD Group. Full dimension keys identify facts; labels are presentation only. XML namespace prefixes are irrelevant.
Invalid code/scope conflicts isolate the affected observations; invalid dataset attributes affect every observation. A misplaced Series attribute affects that Series; a misplaced Group attribute affects matching Group-key observations; a misplaced Obs attribute affects that Obs. Unknown Group type or malformed Group key prevents determining its coverage and rejects the DataSet. Structural DSD errors reject before import.
